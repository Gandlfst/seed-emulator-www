# SEED Emulator logo redesign directions

Built-in image editing, based on the supplied SEED Labs logo. No CLI fallback and no active website assets changed.

- `seed-emulator-mesh-v1.png`: five interconnected nodes (recommended).
- `seed-emulator-orbit-v1.png`: orbital network.
- `seed-emulator-modular-v1.png`: connected modular components.
- `reference-seed-labs.png`: user-supplied reference, retained for the original SEED wordmark.
- `index.html`: pale-background comparison.
- `comparison.jpg`: browser screenshot of the comparison.

These are concept drafts. All prompts lock the original SEED design, but AI outputs are not guaranteed pixel-identical. A final production lockup should reuse the supplied SEED artwork unchanged.

## Final prompts

### 互联节点

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset: an evolved SEED EMULATOR website logo based on the attached SEED LABS logo.
Input image 1 is the EDIT TARGET, not just a stylistic reference.
LOCKED IDENTITY: The entire original left SEED wordmark is immutable. Copy the red SE and near-black ED from the input unchanged in letter contours, segmented horizontal cuts, proportions, spacing, flat colors and alignment. Do NOT retype SEED using a font, do NOT redesign it, do NOT add an extra bar, and do NOT recolor it.
Editable region: only the right LABS word and the three gears above it. Replace LABS with exactly "EMULATOR" (E M U L A T O R), in a clean geometric near-black #202126 uppercase wordmark. The new word is smaller in height than SEED and aligned to its bottom baseline, following the original LABS visual hierarchy. Extend the canvas horizontally as needed to fit the longer word without squeezing the left SEED. Maintain a modest clear gap between SEED and EMULATOR.
Replace the gears with the small symbol described below, positioned above the beginning of EMULATOR, within the original upper-right symbol zone. It must be secondary to the brand text, not huge.
Style: restrained academic technology brand, flat crisp solid shapes and clean linework. Designed to be readable in a website header at 300-400px wide. No 3D, bevels, shadows, gradients, silver, white lettering, added slogans or decorative particles.
Output one single logo, with genuine transparent background and modest transparent margins, no mockup, panel, label, watermark or baked-in checkerboard. Keep the original SEED unchanged.
Symbol direction A, recommended: replace the three gears with a compact abstract network topology. Exactly five small circular solid nodes connected by six or seven straight black links in a balanced asymmetric mesh, with at least one triangular cycle. Simple medium-weight strokes, no globe circle, no arrowheads, no letters in nodes. The network should suggest internet, satellites and blockchain equally rather than being a literal satellite, coin or computer. Use only near-black for the new symbol and word.
```
