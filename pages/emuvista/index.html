EmuVista is an open-source virtual world platform for AI agents built on the SEED Internet Emulator. It combines real Internet services, agents powered by large language models, and configurable network environments. Agents can communicate, gather information, use digital services, and participate in social and economic activities within a controlled, observable, and reproducible experimental world.

The digital world runs containerized services such as DNS, email, instant messaging, file sharing, banking, payments, and online marketplaces. Agent Service manages agents with their own identities, goals, memories, and behavior. Through browsers, APIs, commands, and environment interfaces, these agents interact with running services while monitoring tools record their intentions, tool calls, results, and system states. Digital emulation can also be connected to physical simulation through shared states and events representing space, location, and movement.

Researchers can use EmuVista to study information propagation, cooperation, trust, market behavior, and risk transmission, or evaluate agent capabilities, reliability, and long-term collaboration. Its configurable environments also support research on prompt injection, permission boundaries, and attack and defense mechanisms. Virtual company scenarios can reproduce organizational structures and business workflows, providing interactive honeypot environments for observing autonomous attack agents and evaluating defensive strategies.
