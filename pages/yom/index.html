Yesterday Once More 将有资料记录的互联网历史事件整理为可重复的实验，供研究人员与学生在受控环境中学习事件是如何发生的，以及其中有哪些经验。

每个案例计划汇集历史背景与参考资料、技术说明、实验环境、观察步骤和预期现象。多个案例可复用配套工具与组件。

项目目前处于初期建设阶段，案例、可运行的示例和环境配置指南正在准备中。

overview: |-

Yesterday Once More is a project for recreating historical Internet attacks and incidents to understand how they happened, how they worked, and what we can learn from them. It aims to turn documented events into repeatable experiments that researchers and students can study in controlled lab environments.

Each case is intended to bring together historical context and references, technical explanations, an experiment environment, reproduction steps, and expected observations. Supporting tools and software components can be reused across cases, helping researchers explore the mechanisms and lessons behind past incidents.

The project is in its initial scaffolding stage. Case recreations, runnable examples, and setup guides are being prepared.
