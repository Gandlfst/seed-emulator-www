Terminals, packet captures, logs, and InternetMap connect what the application experiences to what the network is doing. points: - title: Security education text: Study historical routing and malware incidents in isolated experiments and evaluate defenses and recovery. - title: Protocol and service research text: Reuse the same topology to compare routing policies, DNS and digital trust services, or systems such as SCION, Tor, and IPFS. - id: scale nav: Scale title: Internet-scale topologies lead: SEED can turn public CAIDA observations into runnable topologies.
